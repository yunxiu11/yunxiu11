## 👋 About Me

I'm **Yuxuan Zhou**,finishing bachelor and master degrree at **The University of Queensland (UQ)**.

I work at the intersection of **power electronics**, **renewable energy**, **embedded systems**, and **machine learning** — connecting simulation, control, implementation, and engineering validation.

- ⚡ **Power conversion:** Boost PFC, LLC resonant DC–DC converters, PI/PFM control
- ☀️ **Renewable energy:** PV modelling, MPPT, energy storage, EV charging
- 🧠 **Intelligent energy:** smart-meter load forecasting and uncertainty estimation
- 🔧 **Embedded systems:** STM32, C, FreeRTOS, timers, DMA, I²C and SPI
- 🧪 **Engineering workflow:** modelling, measurement, testing and verification

### 🧠 Research Spotlight — Smart Meter Load Forecasting

My machine-learning research explores **multi-horizon electricity demand forecasting** using smart-meter data, including **Sparse Variational Gaussian Processes (SVGP)**, **Monte Carlo Dropout**, and **K-means load-profile clustering**. Model performance is assessed using **RMSE, MAE, and R²**, with particular attention to predictive uncertainty.
